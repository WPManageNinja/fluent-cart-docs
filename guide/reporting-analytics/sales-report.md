# Sales Report

The **Sales** report is no longer part of FluentCart. Its figures now live in other reports, where each one uses the same net sales rules as the rest of the Reports section.

* **Sales totals, refunds, and taxes:** See the [Revenue Report](/guide/reporting-analytics/revenue-report), which breaks sales down from gross sales to net sales and total sales.
* **Orders, items, and order types:** See the [Orders Report](/guide/reporting-analytics/orders-report).
* **Top-selling products and variants:** See the [Product Report](/guide/reporting-analytics/product-report).
* **Marketing sources:** See the [Order Sources Report](/guide/reporting-analytics/order-sources-report).

Every report now shares the rules explained in [How Reports Count](/guide/reporting-analytics/how-reports-count).
