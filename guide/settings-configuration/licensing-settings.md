# Your FluentCart Pro License

This page covers **your own** FluentCart Pro license — entering the key you bought from fluentcart.com so your store unlocks Pro features.

If you sell licensed digital products through FluentCart and want to see which **customer sites** have activated those licenses, see [Customer License Sites](/guide/settings-configuration/managing-licensing-sites) instead.

## Accessing Licensing Settings

1.  From your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **FluentCart Pro > Settings** in the left sidebar.
2.  Click **License Settings** at the bottom of the settings sidebar. This tab appears only when FluentCart Pro is active.

## Managing Your License

1.  On the **Licensing** screen, you'll see your current license status and details.

2.  To activate or update your license, locate the license key input field.

3.  Enter your license key in the provided field.
4.  Click the **"Verify License"** button to activate your license.

    ![Screenshot of Licensing Settings Tab](/images/settings-configuration/license/licensing-settings-tab.webp)

:::tip Need a License Key?
If you don't have a FluentCart license key yet, you can purchase one from [FluentCart](https://fluentcart.com/).
:::

The screen has no separate save button: **Verify License** saves your key.

## License Status

When your key is valid, the panel confirms **Your license key is valid and activated** and shows the **License Key** (with a copy button) and its **Expires** date. Use the **Click here** link next to **Want to deactivate this license?** to release the key from this site.

If the key has expired, the panel shows **Expired At** and lets you enter a new key or renew (**Click Here to Renew your License**).

## Viewing Activated Sites

Once your license is in use, you can see every domain that has activated one of your products from a single screen. Go to **FluentCart Pro > Licenses** and click the **Sites** button at the top of the screen. The list shows each site's **Active Licenses**, **Products**, **Customer**, **Last Activity**, and **Created At**.

To learn how to filter the list, drill into a single site's detail view, and run common workflows like renewal outreach and license audits, see [Customer License Sites](/guide/settings-configuration/managing-licensing-sites).

## Installing Addons in One Click

With FluentCart Pro active, you can install addons directly from inside FluentCart, with no manual download, ZIP upload, or activation step required.

To install an addon:

1.  Go to **FluentCart Pro > Settings > Features & addon**.
2.  Scroll to the **Plugin Addons** section.
3.  Click the **Install & Activate** button on the addon card you want to install.

FluentCart downloads the addon and activates it for you in a single step. For the full walkthrough, see [Installing Plugin Addons](/guide/settings-configuration/features-addons#installing-plugin-addons-one-click-installer).

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues with your license:

1.  Verify that you've entered the correct license key.
2.  Check your internet connection.
3.  Ensure your license hasn't expired.
4.  Contact [WPManageNinja Support](https://wpmanageninja.com/support) if problems persist.
